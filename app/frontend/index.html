How the system works

  1. Upload a colour fundus photograph (or an already preprocessed one).
  2. Preprocessing crops the black border, enhances contrast (CLAHE) and applies Ben Graham filtering, the same steps used to build the training set.
  3. EfficientNet-B3, trained on APTOS, EyePACS and Messidor images, predicts one of five grades.
  4. Explainability: Grad-CAM attention is compared with lesion candidates, retinal zones and image quality so a clinician can judge whether the reasoning is plausible.
  5. Report: an encrypted PDF can be downloaded or emailed. Results are deleted automatically.
